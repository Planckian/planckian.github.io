Seal a page

Encrypts a block of HTML so it can be committed to the public repository without being readable. Paste the plaintext, choose a passphrase, and copy the four strings into the SEAL object in lab/upcoming.html.

This runs entirely in your browser — no network request is made, and nothing is stored. It protects the words from someone reading the repo. It is not access control: the passphrase cannot be revoked for one person, so do not seal anything that would cause real harm if it leaked.
