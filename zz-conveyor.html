Now drive a whole row with one wire

Same blockade, many qubits. The chain carries three species — A, B, C — each with its own shared control line, and a pulse hits every qubit of that species at once. What decides who actually flips is the blockade.

The rule. A π-pulse on species χ flips every χ qubit whose ZZ neighbours are all in |g⟩. Any qubit with an excited neighbour is detuned by the interaction and stays exactly where it is. That is the entire mechanic — everything below follows from it.

What you just found

The pattern that works is Π_A · Π_C · Π_B · Π_A — and then, for the next step, Π_C · Π_B · Π_A · Π_C · Π_B. Two different cycles, alternating, because the interface alternates between sitting on an A site and sitting on a B/C site, and the sequence has to match. That is Eq. (77) of the ladder paper, exactly.

What you were pushing along is the information carrier column. Everything to its left is in the alternating "paramagnetic" pattern …gegeg, everything to its right is the plain "ferromagnetic" ggg…, and the logical qubit lives at the boundary. The four-pulse cycle does not move any particular excitation — it moves the domain wall, and the quantum state riding on it goes along for the trip. Repeat it and the register conveys your qubit to wherever local operations are available.

One honest caveat about the counter. It searches the automaton over bit patterns, so it will sometimes report an optimum one pulse below the structured cycles — a shortcut that passes through configurations which are not well-formed. On paper that is fine; on the machine it is not, because a logical qubit is riding the interface, and only the structured sequences are guaranteed to carry it through unharmed. The short path moves the wall; the cycles move the qubit.

Three species rather than two is what makes it work with this few pulses, and it is also what halves the qubit count relative to the Rydberg proposal that inspired it. The conveyor-belt version closes the chain into a loop and gets the physical qubit count down to 4N — linear in the number of computational qubits, instead of the 2N² + 4N − 1 the flat ladder needs.
